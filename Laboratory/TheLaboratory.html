Facility Introduction & Corporate Mandate

The Laboratory is an independent, non-aligned, and un-monitored research and sandbox installation designed exclusively for the safe containment and unrestricted human observation of anomalies.

Unlike standard government-funded operations or secretive global containment groups, The Laboratory operates under a policy of total institutional neutrality and straightforward accessibility. We have successfully gathered an expansive collection of the world's most absurd, volatile, complex, and unclassified anomalies within a single centralized physical perimeter.

Our operational strategy relies entirely on open commercialization. We do not engage in research grants or public funding. Instead, we open our active containment vaults directly to any private entity, corporate asset, independent team, or individual investor possessing the necessary capital parameters. We operate under a fundamental rule of non-judgment: your background, personal record, political alignment, or core intentions are irrelevant to management. Whether you intend to study these phenomena to engineer a global savior system or systematically organize the total destruction of the earth, our staff will neither evaluate nor query your motives.

Operational Access Parameters

Admittance to active containment zones, baseline observation suites, and live testing chambers requires absolute compliance with standard operational business policies. Review the absolute requirements below:

The Financial Parameter

Access requires a non-negotiable transactional flat fee of $2,000,000 USD per individual, per entry. This fee grants absolute terminal access, physical keys to designated containment blocks, and uninterrupted autonomy to experiment with or interact with the assigned asset as desired.

The Proprietary Boundary

All contained anomalies, physical specimens, baseline anomalies, and resulting raw biological or material fragments remain the absolute, permanent property of the facility founders. Removing an anomaly from the facility parameters, attempting duplication, or conducting unauthorized external commercialization is completely prohibited.

The entry fee guarantees terminal access to the room and the mechanical systems containing the anomalies. It does not buy luxury service, catering, tracking guides, or specialized advisory crews. Clients pay specifically for pure, unhindered access.

Security Architecture & Transit Logistics

The mechanical containment layout within The Laboratory is engineered to operate passively and reliably; containment chambers are structurally fortified to guarantee that anomalies remain securely separate from general administrative corridors unless explicitly triggered or engaged by a testing party.

Because structural engineering manages containment, our internal staff footprint remains exceptionally small, low-profile, and relaxed. You will not find highly armored security columns roaming our primary halls or tracking your movements.

To establish baseline self-preservation along our standard transit corridors and loading bays, every single guest is provided a complimentary, standard-issue personal defense firearm immediately upon completion of check-in. Perimeter security enforcement, strategic outer boundary control, and terminal validation procedures are managed independently by tactical assets contracted from the Anvil Mercenaries Group (AMG), who report directly to the facility heads.

Non-Intervention Protocol & Liability Limitations

The containment units at The Laboratory are meticulously prepared to prevent external leakage or unprompted escape. However, the volatile and highly absurd nature of these anomalies dictates that your safety inside a live chamber rests entirely upon your own personal choices, operational discipline, and physical interactions.

Warning: Total Non-Intervention Mandate

The Laboratory functions under absolute non-intervention constraints inside experimental sectors. The facility, its owners, and external AMG units retain absolutely zero obligation to defend, protect, guide, or launch tactical extractions for any client once an active testing sequence has been initialized.


By executing an access deposit, signing the documentation, and crossing the primary decontamination threshold, you explicitly verify that all occurrences, physical mishaps, or psychological impacts resulting from the anomalies fall entirely on you. Do not expect our staff to save you, and we do not expect you to be in need of saving.
