Facility Introduction

The Laboratory specializes in the straightforward housing, containment, and unmonitored independent testing of anomalies.

  • Absolute Neutrality: We do not ask or care about your background, credentials, or affiliations.
  • Zero Intent Checking: Whether your ultimate goal is to save the earth or completely destroy it, we do not monitor or judge your motives.
  • Open Access: If you possess the capital to secure an entry tier, the anomaly containment suites are completely at your disposal.

Operational Access Parameters

Access parameters are completely transactional and strictly enforced:

The Fee

$2,000,000 USD per person, per entry. Grants total unmonitored isolation with your selected anomaly.

The Rule

All assets remain the absolute property of the founders. No off-site extraction, reproduction, or selling allowed.

Security & Logistics

  • Passive Containment: Structural architecture holds the anomalies secure. Internal personnel environment is highly casual and relaxed.
  • Self-Defense Asset: All authorized entries include a complimentary personal defense sidearm issued upon check-in.
  • Perimeter Defense: Main checkpoints and physical outer boundaries are enforced by contracted agents from the Anvil Mercenaries Group (AMG).
