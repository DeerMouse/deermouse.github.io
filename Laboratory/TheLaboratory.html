Facility Introduction

Welcome to the official index portal. The Laboratory is a privately owned environment dedicated entirely to the straightforward housing, containment, and unmonitored testing of raw anomalies.

  • Policy of Absolute Neutrality: We operate entirely outside of corporate or governmental oversight. We do not evaluate your background, history, credentials, or past affiliations. Admittance is decided purely on transaction validation.
  • Zero Intent Evaluation: Your core intentions are entirely your own business. Whether you are accessing our anomalies to discover a groundbreaking global savior system, or planning to systematically blueprint the absolute destruction of the earth, our staff will not bother to ask.
  • Unrestricted Sandbox Access: Once admitted into your designated testing sector, you have the anomaly entirely to yourself. You are granted complete structural autonomy to test, observe, and experiment with the assets exactly as you see fit.

Operational Access Parameters

Admittance to active containment blocks is handled strictly on a commercial basis. Review our non-negotiable operational boundaries below:

The All-Inclusive Entry Fee

$2,000,000 USD per person, per entry. This flat rate covers authorization profiles and terminal key access to the containment vault. We do not provide hand-holding, guided tours, luxury catering, or dedicated testing advice. You pay strictly for raw access.

The Proprietary Boundary Rule

All contained anomalies, physical specimens, or biological remnants remain the absolute, permanent property of the facility founders. You are strictly prohibited from moving assets outside the physical perimeter of the facility, and you cannot sell them under any circumstances.

Security Architecture

The Laboratory approach to operations cuts out the bureaucratic weight of traditional containment networks. Our facility is designed to be highly straightforward:

  • No Active Security Presence: The facility maintains absolutely zero armed guards, defense forces, or security patrols. The internal atmosphere is completely casual and operates like a relaxed day at the beach, leaving you to focus entirely on your chosen assets.
  • Passive Isolation Engineering: Safety is maintained entirely through passive containment chambers. These units are heavy-duty, meticulously structured cages built to ensure anomalies simply cannot escape into transit walkways or public lounges on their own.
  • Self-Defense Asset Allocation: Because there are no facility guards to step in and protect you if something goes sideways inside a room, all incoming clients are handed a complimentary sidearm weapon immediately upon entry to provide a baseline layer of personal self-defense.
  • The Contractor Role: This specific firearm handout is managed by agents from the Anvil Mercenaries Group (AMG) hired by the owners. They do not protect the facility, they do not patrol checkpoints, and they do not rescue clients; their sole purpose is to process and distribute the equipment.

Non-Intervention & Liability Obligations

Before initializing entry procedures, clients must completely understand their position within the facility parameters:

Warning: Ultimate Personal Accountability

The chambers are explicitly built to keep the anomalies in, but whether they cause severe physical harm or total termination depends entirely on how you choose to interact with them. If you do something stupid, or if a prior containment sequence goes wrong inside a chamber, things can get highly volatile fast.

The Laboratory functions under absolute non-intervention constraints. The facility holds zero obligation to defend, protect, or launch emergency tactical extractions for you once your testing window begins. By completing the contract, you recognize that your survival is entirely your responsibility.

Do not expect us to save you, and we do not expect you to be in need of saving.
