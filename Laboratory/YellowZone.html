The Yellow Zone

Officially classified within structural blueprints as the "No Dead Zone," the Yellow Sector houses entities strictly vetted to lack the biological capacity or psychological intent to inflict fatal trauma upon a subject.

The Illusion of Safety

Clients frequently misinterpret the "No Dead Zone" classification. Survival does not equate to safety. The lack of lethality within this sector arguably presents hazards far more terrifying than outright mortality. The entities housed here do not kill; instead, they alter, trap, and eternally incapacitate.

Warning: A standard ballistic sidearm provided by the Anvil Mercenaries Group (AMG) is virtually useless against structural transfiguration.

If an anomaly permanently turns your flesh into solid stone, or weaves an unbreakable ethereal cage around your physical body, you will not die. However, you will never leave. Under the facility's strict non-intervention waiver, the founders—Z and Light—will not mount a rescue mission to extract you, nor will AMG contractors be dispatched to break your chains. You simply become a permanent fixture of the Yellow Zone's architecture.

The "Lab Rat" Sandbox Philosophy

Unlike traditional containment networks that seek to neutralize, weaponize, or exploit anomalies for profit and salvation, The Laboratory operates under a chillingly neutral paradigm. We do not extract value. We only facilitate curiosity.

The "LR" Designation

Anomalies in this sector are designated with the "LR" (Lab Rat) prefix. This strips them of mythical reverence. They are not world-ending threats; they are raw property placed inside a sandbox for paying clients to poke, test, and observe without consequence.

The Ultimate Reversal

Clients pay millions to be the scientists, viewing the anomalies as their "Lab Rats." Yet, the moment a client miscalculates and is permanently incapacitated by a non-lethal anomaly, the roles reverse. The human becomes the captive test variable.

To our staff, eternal human suffering is merely another data variable in an ongoing experiment. The Yellow Zone is the ultimate playground because non-lethality allows for the longest possible window of uninterrupted experimentation. You can prod a puzzle box for hours, but if the puzzle box turns you into a statue, Junior Researcher Min Kuznetsov will merely log it as a successful curiosity metric.

Containment Audit: LR-002

LR-002 Status: Active
Lethality Metric

Absolute Zero (0.0%). No physical tissue destruction observed in any test parameter.

Incapacitation Mechanism

LR-002 utilizes localized spatial folding to instantly manifest inescapable, heavy-gauge physical chaining around a target's limbs. The chains possess no keyholes, structural weaknesses, or thermal vulnerability. Once attached, they are permanent.

Staff Notes

"Client #412 thought shooting it would break the lock. Instead, the entity just wrapped the gun in chains too. Client has been sitting in Sector 4 for three weeks now. I drop by sometimes to collect data on starvation timelines." — Min Kuznetsov.

Containment Audit: LR-010

LR-010 Status: Active
Lethality Metric

Absolute Zero (0.0%). Biological functions are frozen, not terminated.

Incapacitation Mechanism

Rapid biological calcification. Upon tactile contact or severe provocation, LR-010 initiates an irreversible transfiguration process, converting organic cellular structure into high-density metamorphic stone. The subject's consciousness remains allegedly intact within the stone casing.

Staff Notes

"We had to ask AMG contractors to bring in a forklift last Tuesday. A client got fully petrified right in front of the primary walkway and was blocking the janitorial carts. We just moved him to the corner of the room." — Professor Ton.

Containment Audit: LR-012

LR-012 Status: Active
Lethality Metric

Absolute Zero (0.0%). The entity explicitly refuses to let subjects perish.

Incapacitation Mechanism

Conceptual cage trapping. LR-012 physically warps the architectural dimensions around the subject, creating an invisible, impenetrable cubic barrier. The subject can see the outside world perfectly, and clients can walk right past them, but the subject cannot cross the threshold. The barrier sustains the subject's baseline hydration and caloric needs indefinitely, ensuring they never succumb to natural death while trapped.

Staff Notes

"It's the ultimate observation tank. Founder Z authorized a client to throw paint at it just to see what would happen. The paint hit the barrier and stained it blue. Now we have a blue, screaming box in the middle of the containment wing." — Founder Light.
