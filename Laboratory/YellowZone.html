The Yellow Zone

Welcome to the "No Dead Zone." All entities (Lab Rats) classified under this operational sector possess a 0.0% lethality metric. They lack the biological capacity or psychological intent to inflict fatal trauma upon human targets.

Warning: The Illusion of Safety

Do not mistake a non-lethal classification for safety. The lack of lethality within this sector presents hazards far more permanent than mortality. Yellow Zone anomalies do not kill; they alter, trap, transfigure, and eternally incapacitate.

Standard ballistic sidearms provided by the Anvil Mercenaries Group (AMG) are entirely ineffective against spatial or structural transfiguration.

If you are permanently petrified, encased in an unbreakable ethereal cage, or biologically altered beyond human recognition, The Laboratory assumes zero liability. As stated in your entry waiver, AMG contractors will not mount an extraction, and founders Z and Light will not intervene. You will simply become a permanent fixture of the Yellow Zone's architecture.
