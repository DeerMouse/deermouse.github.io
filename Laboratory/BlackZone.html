The Black Zone

Welcome to the Catastrophic Scale Sector. You are currently viewing profiles for entities that do not care about individual targets. If a containment breach occurs here, the resulting disaster will collapse the facility, warp the surrounding environment, and erase entire communities at large.

Warning: Zero Contingency

The Red Zone targets you; the Black Zone targets the structural reality around you. We do not recommend initiating physical testing or direct observation on any entity housed in this sector. However, because you have paid the entry fee, experimentation is still legally permitted.

Be advised: If a catastrophic chain reaction begins, there is nothing you can do, and there is nowhere you can hide.

Staff Evacuation Protocol

Clients conducting tests in the Black Zone must understand that they are entirely alone. At the first sign of an environmental breach, The Laboratory's internal staff—including Junior Researchers and the AMG contractors stationed at the intake portal—are instructed to immediately evacuate the premises.

They will not wait for you. They will not trigger an alarm on your behalf. They will simply lock the vault doors from the outside and leave the state.

Terminal Notice Regarding Founders Z & Light

If you happen to observe Co-Founders Z or Light conducting their own experiments on a Black Zone entity, WE ARE BEGGING YOU NOT TO COPY WHATEVER THEY ARE DOING. Both founders are structurally insane. If confronted, they will casually inform you that they are "professionals." Do not believe them. You must remain at a distant physical range from their experimentation at all times to ensure your own survival.
