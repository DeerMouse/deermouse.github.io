Containment Vault Directory

Select your testing parameters. The Laboratory divides its structural containment into three primary colored zones based strictly on lethality metrics and scale of threat. Choose carefully; your $2,000,000 entry fee is non-refundable regardless of the zone you select.

Yellow Zone

The "No Dead Zone." Anomalies in this sector have a 0.0% lethality metric. They will not kill you. However, they will alter, trap, transfigure, or eternally incapacitate you. Survival does not equate to safety. A standard sidearm is heavily ineffective against spatial anomalies and permanent petrification.

Red Zone

Active Fatality Sector. Entities housed here possess a singular, aggressive drive to terminate human life. They actively want to kill you. Some fatal encounters can be avoided through strategy; others are mathematically unavoidable. Your complimentary AMG sidearm may save your life here—or it may simply delay the inevitable.

Access Red Vault →
Black Zone

Catastrophic Scale Sector. While the Red Zone targets individuals, the Black Zone affects reality at large. These entities do not merely hunt people; they collapse communities, ecosystems, and environments. If a Black Zone entity breaches its primary passive containment cage, there is no contingency plan. You cannot run, and you cannot fight it.
